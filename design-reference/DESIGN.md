## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intellectual, precise, and contemporary institutional aesthetic tailored for academic and psychoanalytic discourse. Balancing rigor with open, uncluttered legibility, the visual identity draws its foundation from the institutional insignia of the Foro Argentino del Campo Lacaniano (Polo Buenos Aires).

The design movement combines **Contemporary Swiss Modernism** with **Refined Institutional Minimalism**:
- **Geometry & Clarity:** Pure typographic hierarchy, sharp rectangular structures softened only minimally for ergonomic balance, and deliberate structural alignment.
- **Academic Serenity:** Replacing aggressive contrast with nuanced tonal depth—deep cobalt and marine blues evoke clinical authority and institutional prestige, supported by expansive crisp whites and subtle cool-tinted grays.
- **Tone & Mood:** Analytical, dignified, accessible, and cerebral. Interfaces prioritize uninterrupted reading, publication clarity, and structured navigational paths for clinical seminars, cartel announcements, and research archives.

## Colors

The chromatic palette is rooted in the institutional blue lineage extracted directly from the institutional emblem, refined for AA/AAA digital accessibility:

- **Primary (`#1e4b99` / `#2251a3`):** The authoritative cobalt blue used for brand anchors, primary action buttons, active navigation markers, and prominent headlines.
- **Secondary (`#102a56`):** An ultra-deep midnight navy used for high-contrast typography, structural outlines, headings, and dense analytical reading blocks, ensuring total legibility without harsh absolute blacks.
- **Tertiary / Tint Accent (`#e8f0fe`):** A soft cerulean-tinted ice blue used for highlight cards, badges, selected state containers, chips, and subtle focus backdrops.
- **Canvas & Surface Neutral (`#fefefe` canvas, `#f8f9fa` base container, `#f0f4f8` layered surface):** Pristine, cool-tinted off-whites that minimize eye fatigue during extended reading of psychoanalytic texts and seminar programs.
- **Border & Division (`#d4e2f4` / `#e2e8f0`):** Soft, low-contrast lines maintaining discipline across data grids, publication lists, and card modules.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Inter** throughout all tiers to enforce modern objectivity, high mechanical clarity, and unyielding legibility across complex theoretical titles and editorial abstracts.

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in bold and semi-bold cuts with negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.02em`). This yields a compact, architectural rhythm reminiscent of modernist publications and institutional monographs.
- **Body:** Calibrated to an editorial line height (`1.6` to `1.65`) in order to optimize comfort for long-form theoretical papers, clinical case announcements, and seminar syllabi.
- **Labels & Metatags:** Crisp medium/semi-bold treatments with subtle positive tracking to distinguish administrative metadata (dates, authors, cartel categories, polo chapters) from body prose.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces a disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid structured around 8px base grid increments.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, max-width constrained to `1280px`, with `gutter: 1.5rem` and outer margins of `3rem` allowing generous negative space that reinforces academic poise.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, outer margins of `2rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, with outer margins reduced to `1.25rem`. Complex academic listings collapse from horizontal tables into structured card blocks.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections are partitioned with `space-xl` (40px) or double increments (80px), generating breathing room that separates editorial sections, seminar schedules, and institutional documentation without visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy, skeuomorphic, or dramatic dropped shadows, preferring **tonal surface stacking** combined with **subtle low-contrast outlines**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Base background `#fefefe`.
- **Layer 1 (Sub-containers / Panels):** Solid surfaces in `#f8f9fa` framed with a hairline border `1px solid #e2e8f0`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Cards / Floating Dropdowns):** Solid `#ffffff` elevated by an ambient tinted shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(16, 42, 86, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(16, 42, 86, 0.03)` with a delicate border `1px solid #d4e2f4`.
- **Active / Focused States:** Accentuated by an internal or outline halo of `2px solid #1e4b99` rather than high-elevation lifts.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft / Precision Minimal** standard (`roundedness: 1`). 

- Default elements (buttons, inputs, seminar tags, modal sheets) use a refined radius of `4px` (`0.25rem`).
- Larger composite containers and card panels use `rounded-lg: 8px` (`0.5rem`).
- Badges and micro-status indicators utilize subtle pill caps when categorical distinction is required, but structural UI strictly maintains clean, crisp, near-architectural corners that align with the geometric logo geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled background in `#1e4b99`, text in `#ffffff`, border radius `4px`, padding `10px 20px`. Hover transitions to `#153975` with subtle 150ms ease.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #1e4b99`, text `#1e4b99`. Hover fills with `#e8f0fe`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#102a56`. Hover transitions to `#f0f4f8`.

### Badges & Chips
- Category labels for seminars, study days, and cartels: Background `#e8f0fe`, text `#102a56`, font `label-sm`, border radius `4px`, padding `4px 10px`.

### Cards & Modules
- Primary institutional cards feature a `#ffffff` or `#f8f9fa` surface, `1px solid #d4e2f4` perimeter, and inner padding of `1.5rem`.
- Event/Seminar cards feature an accent left border (`3px solid #1e4b99`) to anchor reading flow and quickly signal active academic cycles.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- Input fields: `#ffffff` surface, `1px solid #cbd5e1` outline, `4px` corner radius, typography in `body-md` (`#102a56`).
- Focus state: Border color becomes `#1e4b99` with a subtle outer glow `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(30, 75, 153, 0.15)`.
- Checkboxes and radios: Accent fill in `#1e4b99` on check, with square or circular forms framed in `#102a56`.

### List & Academic Indexes
- Publication and bibliographic lists use alternating rows (`#ffffff` and `#f8f9fa`) separated by thin divider lines (`1px solid #edf2f7`), with interactive states highlighting the text to `#1e4b99`.
